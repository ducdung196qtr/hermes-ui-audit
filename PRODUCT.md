# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js on Vercel for the client-facing product, with a constrained VPS audit controller for browser-based analysis.

## Users

Website owners and small agencies who need a clear, evidence-based UI/UX quality review that they can understand or share with a client.

## Product Purpose

The product turns a public website URL into a structured audit of visible design consistency, responsive behavior, accessibility basics, performance indicators, and SEO foundations. Success is a report that makes the next improvements clear without making unsupported ranking or conversion promises.

## Positioning

A single Hermes browser reviewer checks one website at a time, examining the rendered website at desktop and mobile viewports rather than only parsing source HTML or generating a generic AI response.

## Operating Context

A visitor submits a public website URL, sees an honest FIFO queue position and audit phase, then reads a client-ready report. The scan does not authenticate, submit forms, buy, delete, or send messages on the target website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Strict FIFO queue: exactly one active browser audit; up to three waiting jobs.
- Screenshots and issue crops expire within 24 hours; raw DOM/style payloads are not retained.
- Report summaries expire after seven days for the MVP.
- Public URL scans distinguish observable findings from source-code findings that require authorized repository access.
- English-first UI for global SEO; Vietnamese localization can be added later.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer testimonials, benchmark claims, certifications, or ranking guarantees are available. The product must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before opinion.
2. Explain impact and a practical next action.
3. Keep customer-facing reports clear while preserving technical boundaries.
4. Protect the existing VPS with one-at-a-time execution and bounded resources.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product UI targets keyboard-accessible controls, clear focus states, readable contrast, and mobile use. Audit results are diagnostic guidance, not a WCAG certification.
